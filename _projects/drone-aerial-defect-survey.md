This project focused on the visual workflow of aerial defect surveying, showing how drone-based image capture can support defect identification, remote review, and inspection planning. The emphasis was on making aerial data operationally useful after collection.

## Project Background

Inspection teams often gather imagery successfully but still need a structured review process to turn those images into action. This case was designed to strengthen that post-capture workflow and improve how aerial findings are reviewed and prioritized.

## Technical Highlights

- Aerial image-capture workflow for visible defect survey
- Review-oriented process supporting engineering evaluation and inspection planning
- Remote collaboration workflow using structured visual evidence

## Outcomes and Impact

The project improved visibility into asset condition, reduced the need for repeat site visits, and made remote collaboration more practical during defect review. It also helped transform aerial imagery from documentation into a decision-support asset.

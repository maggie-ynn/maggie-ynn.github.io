This case moved beyond screening and into precise localization. Once abnormal grounding behavior was detected, the work focused on narrowing operational uncertainty and identifying the actual defect location before outage intervention.

## Problem

Abnormal grounding behavior was detected in a 110kV cable system, but the actual fault location remained unclear, creating substantial operational uncertainty.

## Method

Combined live loop resistance measurement with circulating current analysis to identify phase-level anomalies and infer the likely defect location.

## Evidence

- C-phase loop resistance reached approximately 3000 mOhm versus a baseline near 100 mOhm
- Circulating current ratio exceeded the decision threshold of 5
- Fault localized to the cross-bonding grounding section
- Physical inspection confirmed corroded copper braid detachment

## Outcome

- Achieved precise fault localization prior to outage
- Validated diagnosis through post-repair re-testing
- Demonstrated a full closed-loop workflow: detection, localization, and validation
- Eliminated blind maintenance and reduced downtime risk

This case reflects the transition from a single measurement tool to a more intelligent diagnostic capability. The emphasis was not only on field deployment, but on using field feedback to continuously refine the detection system itself.

## Problem

Traditional grounding diagnostics lack real-time feedback and rarely improve iteratively through direct use of field data.

## Method

Integrated research and field deployment by capturing high-resolution measurement data during live testing and using it to refine detection algorithms.

## Evidence

- Night-time field testing campaigns captured edge-case operating data
- Iterative improvement of detection sensitivity and adaptability
- Integration of sensing, data acquisition, and remote analysis

## Outcome

- Established a continuous improvement loop between field deployment and algorithm refinement
- Increased detection robustness under non-ideal conditions
- Transitioned the system from a measurement tool to an intelligent diagnostic platform

This project connected transformer acoustic signatures with a broader inspection workflow so that abnormal operating behavior could be identified and explained more clearly. It was designed as a practical bridge between sensing output and engineering interpretation.

## Project Background

Acoustic signatures can provide valuable clues in transformer diagnostics, but those clues need context before they can guide action. The case focused on interpretation quality, not just signal capture, so that the result could support maintenance and inspection planning.

## Technical Highlights

- Acoustic-signature interpretation for transformer anomaly recognition
- Diagnostic workflow linking sensing outputs with engineering review
- Structured analysis supporting explainable inspection decision-making

## Outcomes and Impact

The case improved the usability of acoustic information in transformer diagnostics and helped translate sensing results into more actionable maintenance insight. It also strengthened communication between technical specialists and field teams during anomaly review.

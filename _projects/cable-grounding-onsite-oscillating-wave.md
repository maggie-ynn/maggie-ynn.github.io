This case extended insulation diagnostics into a harsh deployment environment where altitude directly affects equipment performance and testing stability. The work focused on demonstrating that advanced PD testing remained practical under those constraints.

## Problem

High-altitude environments above 4500 meters degrade insulation performance and challenge conventional testing methods because of reduced air density and limited cooling capability.

## Method

Applied oscillating wave partial discharge testing using resonant charging-discharging cycles to excite defect signatures in cable insulation.

## Evidence

- Successful deployment across multiple substations from 220kV to 500kV
- Stable PD signal acquisition at 4500 to 4700 meters altitude
- System performance maintained despite environmental constraints

## Outcome

- Extended the applicability of PD diagnostics to extreme environments
- Validated the robustness of the resonant-based testing approach
- Enabled reliable insulation assessment in regions that were previously difficult to test

Partial discharge is not directly observable. In transformer diagnostics, it must be inferred from acoustic and spectral signatures that separate one abnormal mechanism from another.

## Problem

Transformer anomalies such as partial discharge, DC bias, and mechanical looseness are difficult to differentiate using conventional monitoring signals.

## Method

Applied acoustic fingerprint analysis combined with AI-based feature extraction using MFCC and pattern recognition to classify fault signatures.

## Evidence

- Distinct harmonic patterns, including odd harmonics associated with DC bias
- Frequency-domain and cepstral feature separation
- Multi-class classification across partial discharge, overload, and structural looseness

## Outcome

- Enabled non-intrusive multi-fault discrimination
- Converted acoustic signals into structured diagnostic features
- Established a foundation for AI-driven condition monitoring

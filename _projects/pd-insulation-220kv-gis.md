This case demonstrates the most complete PD workflow in the group: signal capture, diagnosis, intervention, and verification. The value came from proving not only that a defect existed, but that the corrective action actually removed it.

## Problem

Partial discharge was detected in GIS, but the severity and source location were initially unclear.

## Method

Installed UHF sensors for 24/7 monitoring and compared pre-maintenance and post-maintenance signal characteristics.

## Evidence

- PD amplitude reduced from about -30 dB to -75 dB after replacement
- PRPS pattern disappeared after repair
- Clear before-and-after signal contrast

## Outcome

- Confirmed the defect source as arrester failure
- Validated repair effectiveness through data
- Demonstrated a full diagnostic loop from detection to intervention to verification

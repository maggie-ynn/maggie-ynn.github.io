This project focused on the presentation and interpretation layer of multi-modal inspection, showing how sensing outputs can be organized into a more readable defect-triage workflow. The objective was to make hidden asset conditions easier to review, communicate, and prioritize.

## Project Background

Field diagnostics often produce fragmented outputs from different sensors, which can make defect review slower and less consistent. This project addressed that challenge by structuring multi-sensor information into a more intuitive visual workflow for engineering use.

## Technical Highlights

- Visualization workflow designed for multi-sensor inspection outputs
- Defect-triage framing that supports prioritization and engineering review
- Image-led presentation approach improving communication across inspection teams

## Outcomes and Impact

The project improved communication between sensing teams, operators, and maintenance decision makers by making diagnostic outputs easier to understand and compare. It also increased the practical value of multi-modal inspection by supporting clearer triage decisions.

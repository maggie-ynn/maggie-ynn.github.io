Grounding defects in high-voltage cable systems are often invisible during normal operation, yet they can materially elevate latent failure risk. This case focused on making those hidden conditions measurable without taking the system out of service.

## Problem

Hidden grounding defects in high-voltage cable systems, including loose connections and cold solder joints, are difficult to detect under energized conditions, even though they account for a significant share of latent failure risks.

## Method

Deployed live loop resistance measurement across multiple 110kV cable circuits to evaluate grounding continuity and contact integrity without service interruption.

## Evidence

- Systematic screening across more than 50 cable lines
- Measurable resistance deviation indicating contact degradation
- Correlation between resistance anomalies and physical defects, including poor sealing and joint deterioration

## Outcome

- Enabled non-intrusive, large-scale risk screening
- Shifted grounding diagnostics from periodic inspection to continuous condition awareness
- Established a data baseline for predictive maintenance

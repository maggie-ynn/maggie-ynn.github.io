This case used acoustic imaging to locate hidden defects in a 10kV cable branch box where traditional visual inspection alone could not provide enough clarity. The work showed how sound-based evidence could be turned into a more intuitive diagnostic workflow for field teams.

## Project Background

Cable branch box defects can be difficult to isolate quickly, especially when symptoms are intermittent or not visually obvious. This project was designed to improve fault localization speed and to make the diagnostic result easier to understand in practice.

## Technical Highlights

- Acoustic imaging applied to 10kV cable branch box diagnostics
- Visualization workflow translating sound signatures into fault evidence
- Field-friendly defect localization process supporting maintenance action

## Outcomes and Impact

The case improved speed and confidence in defect localization and helped reduce ambiguity during field troubleshooting. It also showed the operational value of converting non-visual sensing into engineering evidence that teams can act on immediately.

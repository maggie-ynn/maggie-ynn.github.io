This case focused on perception rather than single-sensor detection. The central objective was to combine visual and thermal information so that diagnosis could happen at the equipment level instead of through generic anomaly spotting.

## Problem

Single-modality sensing cannot reliably detect diverse equipment faults in complex substation environments.

## Method

Used a dual-camera system with visible and infrared channels, together with pixel-level alignment and instance segmentation via Mask R-CNN.

## Evidence

- Pixel-level alignment between visible and thermal images
- Instance segmentation for equipment-level targeting
- Thermal anomaly detection on segmented regions

## Outcome

- Enabled device-specific fault diagnosis
- Improved detection accuracy through multi-modal fusion
- Established a perception foundation for autonomous inspection

This case treated partial discharge as a trend-recognition problem rather than a single inspection snapshot. Persistent monitoring made it possible to observe activity windows and distinguish meaningful risk patterns from isolated noise.

## Problem

Intermittent partial discharge in switchgear is difficult to capture through periodic inspection, which leads to missed early-stage defects.

## Method

Deployed multi-channel continuous monitoring over 46 days with synchronized PD signal acquisition and trend analysis.

## Evidence

- More than 120 discharge events recorded across channels
- Amplitude range from -44 dBm to -32 dBm
- Temporal clustering indicating a defect activity window
- Channel-based spatial inference

## Outcome

- Identified floating discharge risk before failure
- Demonstrated the value of time-series PD monitoring
- Enabled condition-based maintenance instead of periodic inspection

This case highlighted an integrated acoustic and infrared inspection platform developed to make field screening more efficient and defect verification more convincing. It brought together complementary sensing modes in a single inspection workflow.

## Project Background

In many utility inspections, a single sensing mode is not enough to confirm whether an anomaly is real, evolving, or maintenance-relevant. This project addressed that gap by combining acoustic and thermal evidence in one deployable platform.

## Technical Highlights

- Integrated acoustic and infrared sensing in one inspection platform
- Multi-sensor workflow for faster screening and stronger defect confirmation
- Field deployment design supporting practical use in transmission and substation environments

## Outcomes and Impact

The platform improved inspection efficiency and made defect verification more robust by reducing reliance on a single data source. It also supported faster field decisions by giving engineers multiple evidence channels in one pass.

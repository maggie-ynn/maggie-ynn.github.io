This case centered on execution under field time pressure. Rather than treating the line as one broad maintenance problem, the objective was to resolve several distributed defects quickly enough to fit within a constrained outage window.

## Problem

Multiple grounding defects were distributed along a transmission cable, requiring rapid identification and resolution within a limited outage window.

## Method

Applied high-resolution loop resistance detection with segment-level analysis to isolate defect locations under constrained field conditions.

## Evidence

- Identified four discrete defect points associated with lead seal cracks
- High repeatability between measurement results and physical verification
- Integrated testing and repair workflow completed within the outage window

## Outcome

- Reduced outage duration through targeted intervention
- Improved maintenance efficiency by avoiding full-line inspection
- Demonstrated scalability of the localization technique in real grid conditions

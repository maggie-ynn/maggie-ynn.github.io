## Impact Highlights

- 10x inspection efficiency vs manual patrol
- Weak PD detection under high-noise conditions
- Real-time spatial localization (not point detection)
- On-board intelligent diagnosis (PRPD-based classification)

## Problem

Manual inspection fails to detect weak PD signals at scale.

## What I Built

An airborne acoustic imaging system enabling wide-area PD mapping through UAV-mounted ultrasonic sensing and real-time processing.

## Evidence

- 62° wide field-of-view acoustic imaging
- 25 FPS real-time mapping
- Hybrid acoustic filtering for noise suppression
- PRPD-based discharge pattern recognition

## Outcome

- Enabled rapid large-scale screening of transmission assets
- Transformed PD detection from point inspection to spatial mapping
- Significantly improved inspection efficiency and coverage

This project focused on turning sensitive electromagnetic measurements into an operational diagnosis workflow. The emphasis was on internal discharge detection that could run continuously in real substations with minimal installation burden.

## Problem

Internal insulation defects in switchgear require high-sensitivity detection with minimal installation constraints.

## Method

Used UHF-based embedded sensors with real-time signal acquisition and classification for internal partial discharge detection.

## Evidence

- Discharge amplitude near the -40 dBm range
- Repeated detection across multiple timestamps
- Consistent internal discharge signature

## Outcome

- Achieved real-time early warning of insulation defects
- Validated the UHF method for internal PD detection
- Enabled scalable deployment in operating substations

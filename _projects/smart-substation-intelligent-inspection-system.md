This project presented an intelligent inspection platform concept aimed at improving inspection consistency, operational awareness, and coordination across digital inspection workflows. The emphasis was on platform thinking: one system coordinating data, inspection, and action.

## Project Background

As inspection programs scale, utilities need more than isolated devices or dashboards; they need a platform that standardizes how information is collected, reviewed, and acted upon. This case was developed from that operational need.

## Technical Highlights

- Platform concept for automated substation inspection and operational coordination
- Inspection, monitoring, and visibility functions framed as one deployable system
- Architecture intended to support scalable and standardized field execution

## Outcomes and Impact

The concept supported more scalable inspection automation and a more standardized view of site conditions. It also created a stronger basis for integrating multiple inspection workflows into one operational platform.

I am a Senior Electric Engineer and Dual-PhD Researcher with over 16 years of experience in high-voltage power systems, energy storage, and intelligent grid operations. Currently serving as a Visiting Researcher in Clean Energy Systems at the University of Waterloo (2023-2025), I specialize in smart grid integration, AI-driven diagnostics, and large-scale R&D project leadership.

My expertise bridges traditional power engineering and cutting-edge AI applications. I have successfully led $100M+ engineering portfolios at State Grid Shanghai, developing brain-inspired perception systems and AI-powered fault diagnosis solutions. My research focuses on applying deep learning (YOLO, R-CNN) and machine learning algorithms to enhance grid reliability, predictive maintenance, and energy system optimization.

## Research Interests

- **High Voltage & Smart Grid**: Ultra-high voltage (1000kV UHV) transmission systems, intelligent monitoring, and automated diagnostics
- **Energy Storage Systems**: BESS integration, NEC 706 & UL 9540/1973 compliance, thermal management
- **AI in Power Systems**: Computer vision for equipment diagnostics, predictive maintenance, fault detection
- **Grid Modernization**: Distributed DC microgrids, EV charging infrastructure, solar-storage integration
- **Carbon & Sustainability**: Carbon accounting platforms, building energy management, renewable integration

## Professional Background

I hold dual PhDs in Intelligent Electronic Information (Fudan University, 2021-2025) and High Voltage & Insulation (Shanghai Jiao Tong University, 2012), along with an M.Sc. and B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from Zhejiang University. As Deputy Director of the Intelligent Maintenance Department at State Grid Shanghai Energy Internet Research Institute (2019-2024), I led a 8 member R&D team in developing innovative solutions for grid intelligence and digital transformation.

My work has been recognized with multiple awards, including the Science and Technology Progress Award from State Grid of China. I am also a Certified Carbon Verifier and serve as Standing Director of IOT sub in the IEEE PES Committee.

**I am actively seeking opportunities in senior engineering roles, R&D leadership positions, and smart grid innovation projects in Canada and North America.**

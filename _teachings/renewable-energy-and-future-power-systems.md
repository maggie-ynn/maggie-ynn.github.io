This course covers renewable energy technologies and their integration into modern power systems, including wind, solar, and energy storage. It focuses on grid stability, system planning, and operational challenges under high renewable penetration.

## Focus Areas

- Renewable generation technologies and deployment pathways
- Grid stability under high renewable penetration
- Power system planning for future transmission networks
- Operational coordination with energy storage resources

## Application Context

Training content is based on practical engineering experience in high-voltage transmission systems and asset reliability, with emphasis on field-oriented applications.

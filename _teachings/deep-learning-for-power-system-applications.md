This course introduces deep learning techniques and their applications in power systems, including load forecasting, fault diagnosis, and asset condition monitoring. Emphasis is placed on practical implementation and engineering use cases.

## Focus Areas

- Deep learning fundamentals for engineering workflows
- Load forecasting and operational prediction
- Fault diagnosis in transmission and substation assets
- Condition monitoring for equipment reliability

## Application Context

The course bridges AI methods with real-world power system scenarios, highlighting implementable workflows for engineers working on diagnostics and reliability.

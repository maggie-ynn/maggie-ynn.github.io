This course focuses on high-voltage insulation performance and overvoltage phenomena in power equipment. Topics include insulation coordination, lightning and switching surges, grounding, and diagnostic techniques for reliability assessment.

## Focus Areas

- Insulation performance in high-voltage equipment
- Lightning and switching overvoltage analysis
- Grounding and insulation coordination
- Diagnostic techniques for asset reliability assessment

## Application Context

The training draws on practical experience across 110kV-500kV transmission systems, with strong emphasis on engineering diagnostics and field decision-making.
